Weeks 1–3 Focus

→Warm up 5 min before every session: leg swings, hip circles, arm circles, light jog on treadmill.
→First week back after illness: if any session feels too hard, cut volume by half rather than skipping — rebuild the habit first.
→If you miss a session, don't double up — just continue the schedule.
→This block prioritises movement patterns and conditioning over heavy loading — that's exactly what Hyrox demands.
→Once Hyrox sessions start at the gym in Sep/Oct, drop one home session and let the gym sessions replace it.
→To keep average HR above 130: rest periods are short by design — move into position during rest, don't fully stop. If HR drops, add a few extra bodyweight reps in the gap rather than waiting.
→Sleep and recovery matter more than any session at your age.
